# probes/ (frozen)

These are the original working folders from the first full probes (L9/N3688, L10/N5749, L22/N11024, L12/N5397). They are kept as read-only history, and their results were migrated into `graph/contributions/` as `core-…` contributions.

New work does not record anything here. Its record is the contribution pair under `graph/contributions/`. Deep screens still write working files to `probes/<neuron>/` while they run. Those folders are git-ignored; bulky material worth keeping belongs in the library (see DATA_POLICY.md).
